# MEDHĀ IDE — Privacy Policy
Last updated: 23 October 2026 · Contact: kanakprabhakar72@gmail.com

MEDHĀ IDE does **not** collect, store or send any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no ads.

* Your code and settings stay on your computer (settings: `%LOCALAPPDATA%\MEDHA\settings.json`; Linux `~/.local/share/medha`).
* MEDHĀ connects to the internet **only** when you yourself open the Extensions panel (it then searches open-vsx.org) or use the AI panel with a local Ollama server on your own PC (127.0.0.1).
* Programs you write run on your computer with your permissions.
* Children: MEDHĀ is a learning tool and collects nothing, so it is safe for students of any age.
This policy follows India's DPDP Act 2023 and the EU GDPR principle of data minimisation.
